Alpaca FAIR Framework

Small, open tools for research data

The Alpaca brings together tools that work together on FAIR research data. The first member to be published is the Academic Meta Tool; more are on their way.

Part of a family

The Alpaca is the framework; the penguin is the Academic Meta Tool (AMT) and the alligator is Alligator. Each member has its own home, linked below.

Tools

AMT.engine

The Academic Meta Tool as a Python reasoner: weighted relations in RDF, rules that derive new relations, six fuzzy logics.

WebsiteSource codedoi:10.5281/zenodo.23669466

AMT.runner

A small wrapper that fetches the engine and runs the whole pipeline on one Turtle file: validate, reason, export. A template to start your own project from.

Where it comes from

The Alpaca FAIR Framework is developed at LEIZA (Leibniz-Zentrum für Archäologie) and in NFDI4Objects, the consortium for the material remains of human history within the National Research Data Infrastructure.
